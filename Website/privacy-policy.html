Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This policy explains what Solterra collects through this website, why, and what happens to it. It's written to be read, not skimmed past — if anything here is unclear, contact us using the details at the bottom.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details — name, phone number, postcode, and what you're enquiring about, when you submit the quote form.
  • Conversation content — anything you type into the chat widget, so our team (and the AI assistant that first responds) can help with your enquiry.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, general location (city-level, from IP), device type, and which link or ad brought you here, via Google Analytics and Meta Pixel.
  • A session identifier — a random ID stored in your browser (localStorage) so a return visit can continue the same conversation instead of starting over.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote.
  • To route your enquiry to the right team member for high-value or urgent jobs.
  • To measure which marketing (e.g. Facebook/Instagram ads, Google) actually leads to real enquiries, so we spend our advertising budget on what works rather than guessing.
  • To meet our own record-keeping obligations as a licensed trade business.

Where it goes

Enquiry and conversation data is sent to our automation platform (n8n) and stored in our CRM (HubSpot) so our team can follow up. Usage data goes to Google (Analytics) and Meta (Pixel) under their own respective privacy policies — we don't control what those companies do with it beyond the standard advertising-measurement purpose we've configured. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses a session identifier stored in your browser's local storage (not a traditional cookie, but similar in effect) to keep a chat conversation connected across page loads. If analytics is enabled, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel also set their own cookies to measure site usage and ad performance — see their respective policies for details on those specifically.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles (Privacy Act 1988), you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Data retention

We keep enquiry and quote records for as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal, and warranty-support purposes, then delete or de-identify them.

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, email hello@solterra.com.au or call 1300 123 456.
