FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

Straight answers, not sales copy. If it's not here, ask the chat widget or call us.

Do I need council approval for solar or a new roof?
Most standard residential solar installs don't need council approval — your energy retailer/DNSP still needs to approve the grid connection, which we handle as part of the process. Re-roofing sometimes needs a permit depending on your council and whether the roofline or height changes; we check this during your free assessment, not after you've signed anything.
What happens if my roof needs repairs before solar goes on?
We flag it in your free roof health check, quote the repair or restoration alongside the solar quote, and do both with the same crew under the same warranty — so you're never told six months in that the roof needs work after the panels are already up.
How long does installation actually take?
A standard solar-only install is usually done in a day. Add roof work first and it depends on the job — a repair might add a day, a full re-roof more. Your written quote includes a real timeframe, not a rough guess.
What if it rains on the scheduled day?
We don't install roofing or solar in unsafe weather. If it rains, we reschedule to the next available slot and let you know as early as possible — you won't be left chasing us for an update.
What warranty comes with the work?
25-year performance warranty on panels, plus 10-year workmanship warranty on the install. Roofing work carries its own warranty depending on material and scope, confirmed in writing on your quote.
Can I finance the cost?
Yes — most customers pay less per month than their old power bill using a payment plan through Brighte or Plenti, on top of the SRES rebate already applied upfront to your quote.
Which suburbs do you service?
Tell us your postcode in the quote form or chat and we'll confirm straight away whether you're in our service area.
My roof was damaged in a storm — can you help with the insurance claim?
Yes. Storm and hail damage jobs get priority scheduling, and we can provide the condition report and photos your insurer will ask for as part of your claim.
