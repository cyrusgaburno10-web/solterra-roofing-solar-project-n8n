03Standardization check & corrections

Everything below was re-checked this session. Two entries are marked correction — they were wrong in the previous revision and are fixed here.

Correction
ManyChat's free plan no longer works for this. As of a March 2026 pricing change, the free tier caps out at 25 active contacts and 2 channels — down from the 1,000-contact free tier it used to have. That's a sandbox, not something a live business can run leads through. Since Meta requires DM automation to happen on its own surface (you can't redirect a Messenger conversation to Telegram), the fix is to connect the Messenger + Instagram Graph API directly into an n8n webhook — free, and squarely within reach for an n8n user. ManyChat Essential ($14/mo) is the fallback if a no-code layer is preferred over building the webhook.
Correction
HubSpot Free has no workflow automation at all — not a smaller version of it. Confirmed limits: 1,000 marketing contacts, 2 users, 1 deal pipeline, 10 custom properties, 2,000 marketing emails/month, and every chat widget/meeting link/landing page carries HubSpot branding. That means n8n isn't filling a gap next to HubSpot's automation — it is the automation layer, end to end.
Automation: n8n
Self-hosted n8n is free software with unlimited workflows and executions — you only pay for the server it runs on (~$5–7/month on a small VPS or managed host). n8n Cloud starts at $24/month for 2,500 executions if server upkeep isn't wanted. Either way it replaces every Make.com reference from the previous revision, since running both would just be two automation platforms doing the same job.
Telegram, kept primary
Free, unlimited Bot API, fine as the AI agent's backbone. The one planning point: customers won't already have you in Telegram the way some might have WhatsApp, so the funnel needs a deliberate "chat with us on Telegram" prompt — FB/IG bio link, website chat launcher, invoices, vehicle signage.
WhatsApp, optional
Meta moved WhatsApp Business off conversation-window pricing to per-delivered-message billing in July 2025. Service replies inside the 24-hour customer-service window are still free; marketing/utility template sends are billed per message at Meta's category-and-country rate card, plus your BSP's markup (roughly $0.003–$0.01/message on top for most providers). Full config notes in §8.
Website rebuild
WordPress + Elementor (both free) remains the AU trades-site standard, paired with HubSpot's official free WordPress plugin so forms/chat sync to the CRM natively.

STC rebate mechanics (why "act now" is a legitimate urgency angle)

The federal STC scheme phases out by 31 December 2030. The "deeming period" — years of future generation a system is credited for up front — steps down every year: 5 years in 2026, falling to 4 in 2027, 3 in 2028, 2 in 2029, 1 in 2030. The drop from 6 to 5 years on 1 January 2026 already cut the rebate on a standard 6.6kW system by roughly $500–$600 versus 2025. At the current STC spot price (~$35–$42, typically ~$38), a Zone 3 6.6kW system earns about 45 certificates worth roughly $1,710 — landing the typical point-of-sale discount around $2,000–$2,500. This is a real, compounding reason for a lead to book this year rather than next — worth building directly into the AI agent's qualification script and the follow-up drip, not just mentioning once.

05Lead economics — does the funnel pay for itself?

Cost-per-lead and close-rate figures below are sourced. Booking-rate and show-rate are not — they're marked as assumptions to swap for your own numbers once the CRM has real data; they only exist to make the worked example complete.

Sourced benchmarks

Lead cost: roofing Facebook lead ads average $40–$80/lead in 2026 (optimized accounts as low as ~$22); storm-response campaigns run $10–$22/lead in the 72-hour post-hail window — the cheapest lead source in roofing. Australia's all-industry Facebook CPL benchmark opened 2026 at $60.50, moving with global trends but swinging harder month to month; the same trade, platform and year can cost 3× more per lead in a busy Sydney suburb than a regional town.

Close rate (on appointments held): roofing averages 27% overall — shared/non-exclusive leads close 8–20%, exclusive leads 25–35%, experienced reps in active storm markets 35–50%, new reps 10–15%. Solar sits lower: 10–20% overall, 13–17% as a healthy baseline, well-optimized operators 18–25%; new reps 10–20%, reps at 6–18 months 20–30%.

08WhatsApp integration — optional configuration

Not required to launch. Add this only once Telegram/FB/IG/website volume justifies a second chat channel — the qualification logic doesn't change, only the transport does.

Provider
Meta WhatsApp Cloud API direct, or Twilio's WhatsApp API if managing WhatsApp through the same account as SMS backup is preferred.
Trigger
Inbound webhook from WhatsApp → same AI agent endpoint already handling Telegram in n8n. Route by a channel field, not a separate bot.
Where it plugs in
Add a WhatsApp node to the existing n8n workflow alongside the Telegram node — same qualification, same HubSpot writes, same drip and reminder logic. No new automation to build.
Pricing model
Per-delivered-message billing since July 2025 (conversation-window pricing is deprecated). Service replies inside the 24-hour customer-service window stay free; marketing and utility template sends are billed per message at Meta's category-and-country rate, plus your BSP's markup — confirm the current AU rate card before turning on the drip sequence over WhatsApp specifically.
When to switch it on
If a meaningful share of leads reply "do you have WhatsApp?" to the Telegram bot, or once FB/IG ad volume in a market where WhatsApp is the norm justifies the setup time.
